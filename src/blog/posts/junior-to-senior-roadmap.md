The transition from a junior developer to a senior one isn't just about writing more code — it's about writing better code, making architectural decisions, mentoring others, and understanding the business side of engineering.

## Technical Skills

### 1. Master Your Core Language

Pick one language and go deep. Understand memory management, concurrency models, and the standard library inside out.

### 2. System Design

Learn to design systems that scale. Understand load balancing, caching strategies, database sharding, and message queues.

### 3. Testing

Write comprehensive tests — unit, integration, and end-to-end. Understand test-driven development and when to apply it.

## Soft Skills

### Communication

Senior developers spend a significant portion of their time communicating. Learn to write clear documentation, give constructive code reviews, and present technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

### Mentoring

Help junior developers grow. Pair programming, code reviews, and knowledge-sharing sessions are all opportunities to mentor.

## Career Strategies

- **Build a portfolio** of impactful projects
- **Contribute to open source** to gain visibility
- **Write technical blogs** to solidify your understanding
- **Network** at conferences and meetups
- **Seek feedback** actively and act on it

The journey is a marathon, not a sprint. Focus on continuous improvement and the title will follow.
